# 🍍 Ananas Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, maintainers, collaborators, and leaders of the
Ananas community pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, healthy, technically rigorous, and collaborative community.

Ananas is built around open-source software, reproducible engineering,
responsible AI use, cloud-native development, and upstream-first collaboration.
We therefore expect all community members to challenge ideas, architecture,
benchmarks, code, and technical assumptions constructively while respecting the
people behind them.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for the Ananas
community include:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
* Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, experiences, technical
  approaches, tools, providers, and levels of expertise
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback during code review,
  design review, benchmark evaluation, issue discussions, and technical debate
* Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes,
  and learning from the experience
* Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall
  Ananas community and the long-term health of the project
* Supporting reproducibility, transparency, security, responsible AI practices,
  and evidence-based engineering
* Contributing improvements upstream when appropriate instead of creating
  unnecessary fragmentation or duplication
* Helping new contributors understand the project, its architecture, and its
  contribution standards without unnecessary gatekeeping

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of
  any kind
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  credentials, API keys, tokens, private infrastructure details, or other
  sensitive information without their explicit permission
* Deliberately misrepresenting benchmark results, security findings, technical
  evidence, authorship, or contribution history
* Intentionally introducing malicious code, credential exposure, destructive
  automation, or unsafe behavior into the project or its community spaces
* Repeatedly disrupting technical discussions after moderation guidance has been
  provided
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional open-source software engineering setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Ananas community leaders and maintainers are responsible for clarifying and
enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and
fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, harmful, unsafe, or materially disruptive to the project
or its community.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, pull requests, code, wiki edits, discussions, issues,
benchmark submissions, documentation, designs, automation, and other
contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate
reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

Community leaders may also restrict access to project infrastructure,
collaboration spaces, automation, repositories, or other Ananas resources when
necessary to protect contributors, users, credentials, systems, or the integrity
of the project.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all Ananas community spaces, and also applies
when an individual is officially representing the Ananas community in public
spaces.

Examples of Ananas community spaces include GitHub repositories, Issues, Pull
Requests, Discussions, Wiki content, project documentation, benchmark
workspaces, design collaboration spaces, community meetings, contributor
channels, and other communication or development environments operated for the
project.

Examples of representing the Ananas community include using an official Ananas
email address, posting via an official Ananas social media account, submitting
upstream contributions while acting on behalf of Ananas, presenting project work
at an online or offline event, or acting as an appointed project representative.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, unsafe, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may
be reported to the Ananas community leaders responsible for enforcement at
admin@aftabshaukat.me.

Reports involving security vulnerabilities, leaked credentials, exposed secrets,
or infrastructure security concerns should also be handled privately and should
not be disclosed publicly before the issue has been reviewed.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly, fairly, and with
appropriate consideration for the safety of the people and systems involved.

All Ananas community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy, security, and
confidentiality of the reporter of any incident.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Ananas community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in
determining the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code
of Conduct:

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language, unnecessarily hostile
technical communication, minor disruption, or other behavior deemed
unprofessional or unwelcome in the Ananas community.

**Consequence**: A private, written warning from community leaders, providing
clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the
behavior was inappropriate. A public apology, correction, clarification, or
removal of problematic content may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact**: A violation through a single incident or series of actions,
including repeated disregard for project standards, moderation guidance,
security practices, or respectful technical collaboration.

**Consequence**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No
interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with
those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period of time. This
includes avoiding interactions in Ananas community spaces as well as external
channels like social media when those interactions relate directly to the
incident. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including
sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment, deliberate disruption, malicious
activity, intentional exposure of sensitive information, or conduct that creates
a meaningful risk to contributors, users, systems, or the integrity of the
project.

**Consequence**: A temporary ban from any sort of interaction, contribution, or
public communication with the Ananas community for a specified period of time.
No public or private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited
interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, is allowed during this
period.

Access to Ananas repositories, collaboration spaces, automation, or project
resources may also be restricted where appropriate. Violating these terms may
lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community
standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an
individual, aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals,
malicious code or infrastructure activity, intentional credential or private
information exposure, serious security abuse, or repeated violations following
previous enforcement action.

**Consequence**: A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction,
contribution, or representation within the Ananas community.

Where technically possible and appropriate, access to Ananas-managed
repositories, infrastructure, collaboration spaces, automation, and project
resources may also be permanently revoked.

## 🍍 Ananas Community Principle

**Challenge the architecture. Question the benchmark. Improve the code. Respect
the person.**

Ananas favors evidence over hype, reproducibility over unsupported claims,
security over convenience, upstream collaboration over unnecessary
reinvention, and professional disagreement over personal conflict.

Contributors are encouraged to question technical decisions, compare models,
challenge benchmark methodology, propose alternative architectures, and identify
weaknesses in the project. Those disagreements should remain grounded in
evidence, reproducible testing, and respect for the people participating.

For conduct concerns that should not be handled publicly, contact the Ananas
project maintainers at admin@aftabshaukat.me.
